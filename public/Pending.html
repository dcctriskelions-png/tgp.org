Pending Members

Review new applications, then approve or decline them.

Encoded members with no Member ID yet. Each one already has a complete Member ID: 7100 – Chapter Code – Year Initiated – Roll Number (example 7100-001-2006-00001). Check it, then press Approve to give the member that ID, move them to the Master List, and email them the ID. Press Decline to remove the application from this list.
